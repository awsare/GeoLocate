Chosen Configuration

The selected model preserves strong test accuracy while improving geographic quality through a distance-aware objective.

Decision Rationale

  • ResNet34 gave the best accuracy trend among tested backbones.
  • Two-stage fine-tuning stabilized optimization after replacing the classifier head.
  • Class weighting improved minority-sector balance relative to plain runs.
  • Distance penalty at 0.1 improved geographic score without hurting top-line accuracy.
